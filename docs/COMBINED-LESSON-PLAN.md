# Combined 4-Week Lesson Plan — Lucito Learning

**Baseline:** Pre-reader, counts to ~20 (needs one-to-one and cardinality practice). Strong interest in cars/environmental print.

**Daily structure (10–15 min):** 1 mode of the app + offline real-object activity. Parent co-play for first week.

## Week 1: Numbers 1–5 + Letters A–E + Sight Words Week 1
- App: Counting mode (tap each object, hear count, match number) + Letter sounds A–E + Sight words (a, and, away, big, blue...).
- Offline: Count cars/toys, point to letters on signs.
- Goal: Accurate one-to-one to 5; recognize A–E sounds.

## Week 2: Numbers to 10 + Letters F–M + Sight Words Week 2
- Expand counting range. Add more/less comparison.
- Continue sight words progression.
- Offline: Count household items, letter hunts.

## Week 3: Numbers to 15 + Letters N–T + Sight Words Week 3
- Introduce simple addition with objects (two groups).
- Mastery test on Friday for current week words.

## Week 4: Numbers to 20 + Letters U–Z + Sight Words Week 4
- Full review. Friday mastery test.
- Celebrate with extra stars and offline play.

**Progression:** Advance numbers/letters only when ~80% accurate. Dashboard tracks sight words; stars track counting/letters.

**Evidence base:** Science of Reading (letter-sound + phonemic awareness), WWC Teaching Math to Young Children (developmental number progression, daily practice).
