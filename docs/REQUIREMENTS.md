# Requirements — Sight Words Lucito

## Problem
Lucito is 5, pre-reader, counts to ~20. Recognizes environmental print (Texas on signs, Toyota on cars). Needs audio-first sight word practice that feels like play, not school.

## Goals
1. Learn Dolch pre-primer 40 words over 4 weeks (10/week).
2. Every interaction is spoken — no reading required to play.
3. Car-themed visuals and scenarios to leverage his existing interest.
4. Dashboard shows weekly completion and accuracy for Joe to review.
5. Friday mastery test: 80% pass threshold to unlock next week's words.
6. Zero cost, zero accounts for Lucito. One HTML file.

## Non-Goals
- No accounts, logins, or cloud sync (localStorage only).
- No ads, timers, or failure states that feel punitive.
- No text-only instructions — voice covers everything.
- Not a general reading app; sight words only for now.

## Success Criteria
- Lucito can independently open the app and complete a 5-minute session.
- After 4 weeks, he recognizes all 40 words in the mastery test.
- Joe can see per-word accuracy and weekly progress on the dashboard.
